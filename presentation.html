HOW LIFELINE SAVES LIVES (PROCESS FLOW)
1

1. Instant Distress Trigger

Villager or patient presses a single tactile key on the TX Pro handheld to select emergency type (Medical, Maternal, Critical, Evacuation).

2

2. Sub-GHz Radio Propagation

SX1278 LoRa radio broadcasts packet across 10+ km at 433 MHz, bending around mountain ridges and penetrating dense pine forest.

3

3. Base Station Acoustic Alert

RX Pro at ward health post receives beacon, activates loud multi-tone siren, displays caller coordinates on LCD, and flashes status LEDs.

4

4. Two-Way ACK & Cloud Dispatch

Base station transmits encrypted ACK back to the caller's display ("RESCUE DISPATCHED"), then flushes incident to central cloud dashboard.

The Humanitarian Crisis

In remote Himalayan districts like Jumla, Dolpa, and northern Karnali, thousands of families live in total telecommunications blind zones. When medical emergencies or natural hazards strike, the absence of basic communication turns treatable conditions into fatal tragedies.

!
Maternal Mortality: Mothers in labor suffer postpartum hemorrhage and cannot call health post stretchers in time.
!
Delayed Trauma Evacuation: Victims of falls, landslides, or sudden cardiac events die during 12-hour foot journeys.
!
Telecom Infrastructure Failure: Commercial NTC/Ncell towers fail first during monsoons, landslides, and earthquakes.
WHY EXISTING ALTERNATIVES FAIL
✕

Cellular Smartphones & Mobile Apps

100% dependent on commercial mobile towers, electrical grid, and data packs. Completely inoperable in mountain blind-spots.

✕

Commercial Satellite Messengers

Garmin InReach and Iridium cost NPR 50,000–80,000 per unit plus mandatory recurring dollar subscriptions (NPR 4,000/mo) that rural Gaunpalikas cannot sustain.

✓

The LifeLine Difference

Costs under NPR 7,000 per node with zero recurring fees. Operates 100% off-grid on Sub-GHz RF, with battery/solar backup, and provides bidirectional confirmation.

SOFTWARE ARCHITECTURE TIERS
A

Web Bluetooth PWA (Bluefy Suite)

Enables any smartphone to connect directly to LifeLine hardware over BLE Nordic UART through a mobile web browser with zero internet.

B

RESTful API Ingestion Gateway

Handles incoming JSON telemetry packets from RX base stations, normalizes timestamps, verifies auth tokens, and triggers alert pipelines.

C

Operations Command Portal

Provides emergency coordinators with live incident maps, responder dispatch matrices, fleet device status, and exportable logs.

MULTI-TIER SCALABILITY TIMELINE
1

Phase 1: Local RF Community Mesh (Current)

Deploying LoRa 433 MHz networks connecting isolated mountain wards and rural health posts in Karnali/Jumla with zero telecom dependency.

2

Phase 2: Hybrid GSM Auto-Failover

Integrating cellular SIM modules into RX base stations to automatically route telemetry to national headquarters whenever a mobile tower is within range.

3

Phase 3: Direct Satellite Fallback

Equipping regional base stations with low-power direct satellite uplinks (Iridium / Swarm / NTN) for 100% fail-safe coverage in extreme glacial zones.
